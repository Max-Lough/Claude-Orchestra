# Critique
## Steelman
The rival plan separates durable capability contracts from pool-aware model casting, applies hard context-shape constraints, and uses shallow delegation across 24 specialized roles. Cheap agents and deterministic verification handle bounded work, while premium agents address ambiguity, difficult execution, and consequential review. It also preserves the current harness’s integrity mechanisms, introduces explicit verification and allowance-management functions, and conditionally places Gemini in narrow multimodal, UI-reference, and mechanical seats.

## Findings
1. **[BLOCKER] A3 “Synthesizer” and routing row T23 violate the hard family-independence rule.** The plan explicitly accepts that the synthesizer shares a family with one of the two architects and offers blindness and session-by-session rotation as mitigation. Blindness removes identity bias but cannot remove correlated family blind spots; a consequential planning artifact is still being evaluated by its author’s family. Because Gemini is rejected for synthesis, this gate must terminate in qualified human adjudication rather than the acknowledged same-family evaluation.

2. **[BLOCKER] D1 “Reviewer,” §3.3, §5.6, R4, and V4 make a hard requirement optional.** The degraded reviewer row permits same-family gate review, Red mode permits gated work to proceed that way, R4 recommends shrinking the mandatory set under asymmetric subscriptions, and V4 says an unfavorable 20-change trial would collapse the mandate to fresh-context review “of any family.” Disclosure and deterministic checks do not satisfy the brief’s prohibition where correlated blind spots are expensive. The policy would allow Principal, integration, or planning work to close under precisely the correlated review the architecture is required to prevent.

3. **[BLOCKER] The Summary, role catalog, and §4 routing table do not provide the claimed one-to-one task ownership.** Conductor and Architect both claim T22; Synthesizer and Quartermaster both claim T24; Architect’s ordinary planning class is T22 in its catalog but T8 in the table; and T23 names “Architect ×2 + Synthesizer” rather than one primary role. Semantic boundaries also overlap: “works locally, fails in CI” selects both Operator and Bug Hunter. Step 6 postpones resolving these contradictions until after schemas and routing code are built, inviting inconsistent dispatch and failing the hard requirement that every named class have one obvious primary.

4. **[BLOCKER] §5.3 does not supply cost coverage for every task class.** Its table covers only 13 of 24 unique classes; T4, T9–T11, T13–T15, T17, T21–T22, and T24 have no per-round estimate. It also routes on “below reserve” without defining a default reserve threshold, while AU and OU totals are not expressed as shares of the relevant subscription allowance. An implementer therefore cannot construct the specified pool state machine or assess the architecture’s complete quality-per-allowance claim without making new policy decisions.

5. **[MAJOR] D3 “Verifier” and Work-plan Step 4 grant insufficient permissions for the mandatory checks.** The role allows execution but “No WRITE anywhere,” even though builds and test runners commonly write caches and generated artifacts, while the required mutation check necessarily changes an assertion, implementation, or isolated checkout. The current harness documents this exact operational constraint: its review runner defaults to `workspace-write` because many suites cannot run read-only (`packs/codex/hooks/orchestra-review.js:113–116`; `README.md:625`). As specified, the Verifier will either fail closed on routine projects or silently omit the checks on which every substantive review depends.

6. **[MAJOR] P2’s transparent-recasting premise is not honored by several role contracts.** Doc Writer has no opposite-pool mirror; Data Engineer’s Terra mirror covers only the reversible subset; and Archivist’s mirrors cover documents, logs, and images but not the video/audio inputs that justify the Gemini seat. Google’s current model page confirms that video and audio are native Gemini 3.7 Flash inputs and lists Antigravity availability, making this a real capability distinction rather than interchangeable casting metadata. [Gemini 3.7 Flash model information](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/flash/) When Google is unavailable, T19 cannot preserve its contract “without a plan change,” so pool exhaustion remains an outage or reclassification for part of the roster.

7. **[MAJOR] B4 “Detective” leaves consequential causal reasoning structurally unreviewed.** A Verifier can confirm that cited lines exist, but cannot establish that the causal chain is complete, falsify alternative explanations, or detect the role’s named “confident narrative” failure. Since Detective verdicts redirect plans and potentially expensive fixes, using a more capable model is not a substitute for an independent opposite-family challenge. The design invites a well-cited but incorrect diagnosis to become unchallenged ground truth.

8. **[MAJOR] A1, P11, §3.2, and C7 assign irreversible-action authority inconsistently.** Conductor is said to own irreversible-action approval permanently, and Data Engineer requires Conductor approval before apply, while another escalation path says Conductor → user for any irreversible step. An alignment score can justify which model prepares a decision packet; it cannot authorize production migration, credential movement, destructive data work, or release side effects. The ambiguity permits an implementation to treat model approval as sufficient human authority.

9. **[MAJOR] Adjudication 1 over-interprets Terminal-Bench 3.0 and never schedules the validation needed to support the resulting escalation inversion.** The official leaderboard compares Opus 5 under mini-SWE-agent with Sol under Codex—not the same scaffold—and explicitly reports model-agent pairs. [Terminal-Bench 3.0 leaderboard](https://www.frontierbench.ai/) The target repository instead defines Opus as a native Claude-side agent (`agents/executor-heavy.md:5`) and Sol through the Codex runner (`packs/codex/hooks/orchestra-exec.js:188–189`), so the assertion that the benchmark measures “the same pairs” is false. Step 11 contains no hard-environment A/B trial; R5 merely says another trial “can be extended,” inviting an unsupported cross-vendor hop on the hardest incidents.

10. **[MAJOR] Work-plan Steps 13–15 retire the existing roster before system-level validation without a compatibility or rollback path.** Existing protocol and skill artifacts dispatch names such as `executor`, `reviewer`, and `executor-heavy-xhigh`; deleting or archiving them breaks persisted plans and in-flight orders unless aliases or a versioned migration are provided. Re-baselining occurs only afterward, with no shadow phase, risk-tier canary, kill switch, or defined restoration procedure if V1 or V2 fails. This invites a flag-day migration whose first whole-system failure is discovered after the known-good roster has been removed.

11. **[MAJOR] “Evidence register and number sensitivity” does not provide checkable provenance for several load-bearing figures.** Source letters are confidence classifications, not citations: the Gemini 52.1%/16.9% failure decomposition names no analysis, the Anthropic weights cite an unnamed aggregator, the 7× parallel-agent burn is only “via secondary,” and the Codex-window oscillation cites unidentified press. These figures drive role exclusions, fan-out caps, and allowance arithmetic, yet a reviser cannot reproduce or refresh them. This fails the brief’s explicit requirement that numbers affecting casting carry enough source provenance to be checked.

## Comparative assessment
The rival plan is stronger than the supplied own plan in its explicit separation of capability contracts from model casting, its hard context-shape vocabulary, and its named Verifier, Sweeper, and Quartermaster responsibilities. Its repository reconnaissance is also sharper: the direct-API `/deep-plan` dependency, installed-versus-master architect drift, asymmetric web tools, and detective/executor investigation gap are substantiated by the tree. The evidence register’s reversal conditions and the explicit migration of the API-only planning lane are valuable additions despite the citation defects.

The supplied own plan is stronger on hard-policy compliance and executable completeness. It provides exclusive task discriminators, costs every canonical class, defines numerical pool states and review reserves, blocks rather than degrades consequential cross-family gates, keeps irreversible authority human, gives reviewers writable isolated verification space, and uses shadow, canary, aliases, and rollback before retirement. Its smaller roster also avoids several overlapping ownership and multi-author review problems while still separating read-only causality from runtime investigation. The strongest synthesis would retain the rival’s contract/casting abstraction and operational instrumentation while preserving the own plan’s strict review invariant, complete taxonomy and cost table, human gates, and staged migration controls.
