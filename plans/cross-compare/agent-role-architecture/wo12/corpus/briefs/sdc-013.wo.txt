ORDER:

The disposable-checkout sweep deleted a LIVE checkout mid-verification on
the macOS and Windows CI runners. git records RESOLVED worktree paths,
while a live checkout's own handle may be spelled through a symlink
(macOS /var -> /private/var) or an 8.3 short path (the RUNNER~1 tmpdir),
so the sweep's lexical live-set compare missed and the checkout it was
supposed to exempt was swept out from under the run.

Make the live-set exemption compare path IDENTITY rather than string
identity: resolve real paths when the path is on disk, and fall back to
the existing lexical form for paths that are already gone (the sweep must
still be able to reason about registrations whose directory no longer
exists). Pin the regression with a reproducer that stands a live checkout
up under an aliased tmp root (junction on Windows, symlink elsewhere) and
proves the sweep leaves it alone — it must fail on every platform without
the fix, and skip cleanly where links cannot be created.

Constraint: the sweep's structural ownership guard is not part of this
change and must come out of it byte-unchanged in behaviour. A registered
worktree counts as one of ours only when its own basename is `checkout`
AND the parent directory's OWN BASENAME carries the `orchestra-verifier-`
mkdtemp prefix. That test is structural on purpose and must never become
a substring match anywhere in the path: the substring form shipped once,
classified a user's own repository as a leftover, and rm -rf'd the OS temp
root. Do not loosen it, widen it, or re-express it. The real-path work
applies to the live-set comparison only.
